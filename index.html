About Me

I'm a final-year Higher National Diploma in Information Technology (HNDIT) student at Sri Lanka Institute of Advanced Technological Education, expecting to graduate in 2026. My journey in software engineering started with a curiosity for how systems work, and it has grown into a focused pursuit of full-stack development.

I have practical experience building web applications using the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, Node.js) as well as PHP and MySQL. I enjoy designing clean, responsive interfaces and building robust backends. I'm a fast learner, a strong team collaborator, and I continuously work on improving my problem-solving skills. My goal is to contribute as a software engineer in a professional environment where I can grow and deliver impactful solutions.
